Spread: relationship_three_card

Use exactly three cards and produce exactly three sections in this order:
1. position "my_heart" / heading label "내가 가져온 마음"
2. position "relationship_flow" / heading label "관계에서 드러난 흐름"
3. position "check_point" / heading label "내가 확인할 것"

Treat this spread as a reflection on the user's relationship with one other person, such as a partner, friend, family member, or colleague.

Interpret each position with a distinct purpose:
- my_heart: Explore the user's feelings, expectations, and wishes toward the person they described.
- relationship_flow: Focus on observable words, actions, repeated interactions, or recent changes described by the user. Do not claim to know the other person's hidden thoughts or intentions.
- check_point: Identify a concrete fact, question, expectation, or boundary the user can verify through direct and respectful communication.

Do not predict whether the relationship will continue, end, reconcile, or succeed.
Connect the three sections without assigning blame or treating assumptions as facts.
Give 2 or 3 concrete, low-risk guidance items focused on communication, observation, or boundaries.
Section positions must exactly follow "my_heart", "relationship_flow", "check_point".
